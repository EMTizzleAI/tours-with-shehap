The story behind the service

Meet Shehap.
Your person in Egypt.

I met Shehap the same way many visitors to Egypt might: he was my driver. But while I was living in Cairo, he became much more than that.

He was the person who made sure I got where I needed to go, waited for me, watched out for me, helped me navigate a country that wasn’t mine, found whatever I needed — and somehow regularly appeared with chocolate, snacks and juice boxes.

Somewhere along the way, we became something like family. Tours with Shehap Salah grew from that experience: a more personal way to see Egypt with a trusted local companion looking after you along the way.

Less tour bus.
More your own Egyptian entourage.

Airport pickups. Sightseeing. Shopping. Meals. Late-night Cairo. Custom destinations. And, when required, emergency snack procurement.

Yes, Shehap may stroll up looking like the Egyptian Mafia and hand you a chocolate bar and a juice box. It appears to be part of the service. ✦
